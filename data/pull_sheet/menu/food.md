# Food menu and where to find it

The food items Bay Laurel Catering pulls for events, how their names appear on a BEO (Banquet Event Order), and where each one is kept. Use it to answer "where is the X?" for the pull crew.

## Storage locations
- **Hot box**: inside the kitchen.
- **Service cooler**: the walk-in cooler for service items. Some items are kept "near the door to the kitchen".
- **Metal rack, service room**: the metal rack in the service room, near the manager's table.
- **Metal racks, pull area**: the metal racks in the pull area (different from the service room rack).
- **Beverage cooler**: the cooler for beverages.

## Hot breakfast
- **Scrambled eggs with cheddar**: in the hot box inside the kitchen. On a BEO: "Organic, cage-free eggs scrambled with cheddar cheese".
- **Chicken-apple sausage links**: in the hot box inside the kitchen.
- **Roasted breakfast potatoes**: in the hot box inside the kitchen.

## Bakery
- **Breakfast muffins**: on the metal rack in the service room, near the manager's table.
- **Butter croissants**: on the metal rack in the service room, near the manager's table. On a BEO: "Butter croissants served with raspberry preserves". This is also the croissant in the Cascade and Olympic breakfast packages.
- **Raspberry preserves** (served with the croissants): in the service cooler. Pull (bring) them together with the croissants. They have their own location.
- **Fruit danish**: in the service cooler. On a BEO: "Fruit danish assortment".
- **Assorted bagels**: on the metal rack in the service room, near the manager's table. On a BEO: "Assorted bagels with cream cheese".
- **Cream cheese** (served with the bagels): in the service cooler. Pull (bring) it together with the bagels. It has its own location.
- **Assorted scones**: on the metal rack in the service room, near the manager's table. On a BEO: "Assorted flavored scones".
- **Gluten-free vegan pastry**: in the service cooler, near the door to the kitchen.
- **Plant-based herb cream cheese**: in the service cooler.

## Yogurt
- **Greek yogurt**: in the service cooler. On a BEO: "Assorted flavored Greek yogurts", "Creamy 100% natural Greek-style yogurt with nutty granola, fresh berries, and honey on the side", or "Creamy Greek yogurt w/ honey and granola" (in the Cascade Breakfast).
- **Vegan yogurt**: in the service cooler. On a BEO: "Vegan Yogurt - coconut vanilla".
- **Granola** (served with the yogurt): on the metal rack in the service room, near the manager's table.
- **Fresh berries** (served with the yogurt): on the metal rack in the service room, near the manager's table.
- **Honey** (served with the yogurt): on the metal racks in the pull area.

When yogurt is served with granola, fresh berries and honey, pull (bring) all four. Each one has its own location.

## Fruit
- **Whole fresh fruit**: in the service cooler or on the metal rack in the service room.
- **Market fresh fruit tray** (small, medium and large): in the service cooler. On a BEO: "Small/Medium/Large market fresh fruit tray: cantaloupe, grapes, honeydew, pineapple, seedless watermelon, and a variety of seasonally available selections". The "Freshly cut fruit platter" in the Cascade and Olympic breakfast packages is this same item.

## Snacks
- **Trail mix** (bags): on the metal rack in the service room.
- **White cheddar popcorn** (bags): on the metal rack in the service room. On a BEO: "White cheddar popcorn (bags)", sold by the dozen.

## Lunch
- **Specialty sandwich**: in the service cooler.
- **Classic deli sandwiches**: in the service cooler. On a BEO: "Classic deli sandwiches prepared on sliced deli whole wheat, sourdough, and rye breads with lettuce, tomato, cheese, Dijon mustard and mayonnaise on the side".
- **Husky wrap platter**: in the service cooler.
- **Quinoa-chickpea tabbouleh salad**: in the service cooler. It serves 15-20 entrée portions or 30-40 side portions.
- **Box meals**: in the service cooler or in the beverage cooler. A box meal is one item on its own. It comes in four kinds (fresh veggie sandwich (V), roasted turkey with havarti, smoked ham and Swiss, Italian baguette). On a BEO: "Box meal: ...".
- **Bottled water**: in the beverage cooler. It is sometimes pulled as its own item, for example "bring bottled waters for boxed lunches".
- **Tim's Cascade potato chips**: on the metal rack in the service room. They are sometimes pulled as their own item.
- **Dijon mustard**: in the service cooler. Pull (bring) it together with the classic deli sandwiches, where it is served on the side. It has its own location.
- **Mayonnaise**: in the service cooler. Pull (bring) it together with the classic deli sandwiches, where it is served on the side. It has its own location.

## Desserts and cookies
- **Chocolate brownies**: in the service cooler. On a BEO: "Chocolate brownies" or "Chocolate brownies (petite)".
- **House baked cookie assortment**: on the metal rack in the service room.
- **Individual gluten-free/vegan cookie**: in the service cooler, near the door to the kitchen.
- **Gourmet dessert bars (V)**: in the service cooler.

## Breakfast packages
These packages are not stored as items. They expand into the items above.
- **Cascade Breakfast** (per person): butter croissants with raspberry preserves, fresh fruit tray (freshly cut fruit platter), Greek yogurt with honey and granola, and a beverage package (coffee, decaf, tea, iced water and juice), plus orange juice.
- **Olympic Breakfast** (per person): butter croissants with raspberry preserves, fresh fruit tray (freshly cut fruit platter), and a beverage package (coffee, decaf, tea, iced water and juice), plus orange juice.
