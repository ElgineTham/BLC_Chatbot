# Beverage menu

The beverages Bay Laurel Catering serves, and how their names appear on a BEO (Banquet Event Order). This page mirrors `data/pull_sheet/menu/beverages.json`, which the pull sheet program uses. Keep the two in sync when the menu changes.

## What BEO names mean
- "Coffee" on a BEO always means regular (caffeinated) coffee, never decaf.
- "Fresh-brewed coffee" on a BEO always means regular (caffeinated) coffee, never decaf.
- Decaf is always written out as "Decaf" or "Fresh-brewed decaffeinated coffee". If a line does not say decaf, it is regular coffee.
- "Juice" in a package means orange juice, unless the BEO has a separate line for a different juice. Orange juice is the only juice on the menu.
- "Tea" in the coffee, decaf, tea, iced water & juice package means hot water with tea bags.
- "Hot water with an assortment of black, green, and herbal tea bags" is the menu item Hot water.
- "Iced tea with lemon wedges" is the menu item Iced tea.
- Drinks that are not on this menu (for example hot chocolate, espresso, soda or bottled water) have no serving rules here. The pull sheet lists them under "Not used (check by hand)".

## Drinks served by the gallon
Each of these drinks is made by the gallon and served from cambros.
- Coffee (regular coffee) is a hot drink. On a BEO it may also be written "Fresh-brewed coffee".
- Decaf is a hot drink. On a BEO it may also be written "Fresh-brewed decaffeinated coffee".
- Hot water (for tea) is a hot drink. On a BEO it may also be written "Hot water with an assortment of black, green, and herbal tea bags".
- Brewed hot cider is a hot drink.
- Ginger Honeycrisp apple cider is a hot drink.
- Iced water is a cold drink.
- Iced tea is a cold drink. On a BEO it may also be written "Iced tea with lemon wedges".
- Orange juice is a cold drink.
- Lemonade is a cold drink.
- Peach tea is a cold drink.

Hot drinks are served in hot cups. Cold drinks are served in cold cups.

## Add-ons counted each
- Oatmilk is counted in pieces (each), not in gallons.
- For oatmilk, bring the number on the BEO line, rounded up to a whole number.
- Oatmilk does not go in a cambro.

## Package: Coffee, decaf, tea, iced water & juice
The package line "Coffee, decaf, tea, iced water & juice" stands for five drinks: coffee (regular), decaf, hot water (for tea), iced water, and orange juice.
- The juice in this package is orange juice, unless the BEO has its own line for a different juice.
- The quantity on the package line is the number of guests.
- Each of the five drinks is made for that many guests, at 1 gallon per 25 guests.
- Example: the package for 70 guests means 3 gallons each of coffee, decaf, hot water, iced water and orange juice.
- If the BEO also has its own line for a drink in the package, use the larger amount, do not add them.
- Example: the package for 70 guests plus an "Orange juice" line for 70 guests means 3 gallons of orange juice, not 6.
