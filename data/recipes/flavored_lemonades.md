# Flavored Lemonades (Strawberry, Pomegranate, Lavender, Mango)

Per 1 gallon: 25 oz lemonade concentrate, plus the flavor below, plus water to fill the bucket to 1 gallon. Scale linearly for bigger batches.

| Flavor | Flavor amount per gallon |
|---|---|
| Strawberry | 7 oz strawberry syrup |
| Pomegranate | 8 oz pomegranate juice |
| Lavender | 7 oz lavender syrup |
| Mango | 7 oz mango syrup |

Method: pour the concentrate and flavor into a clean bucket, fill with water to the batch size, and stir well.
