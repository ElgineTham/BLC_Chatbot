# Citrus Fruit Punch

## Yield
1 gallon

## Ingredients
- 25 oz pineapple juice concentrate
- 25 oz lemonade concentrate
- 25 oz orange juice concentrate
- Water to fill to 1 gallon
- 24 oz Sprite (added just before serving)

## Method
1. Pour the pineapple, lemonade and orange juice concentrates into a clean bucket.
2. Add water to fill the rest of the bucket to 1 gallon and stir.
3. Just before the punch is served, add 24 oz of Sprite per gallon.

## Notes
- Scale linearly: each gallon takes 25 oz of each concentrate and 24 oz of Sprite.
- Add the Sprite only just before serving so it stays fizzy.
