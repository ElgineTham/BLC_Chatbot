# Flavored Iced Teas (Mango, Raspberry)

Per 1 gallon: 8 oz mango syrup or raspberry syrup, one tea bag, and water. Scale the syrup linearly.

Tea bags: a 1-gallon bag brews 1 gallon; a 3-gallon bag brews 3 to 4 gallons. Use the fewest bags that cover the batch.

Method: place the tea bags in a sanitized bucket and add the syrup. Pour hot water to cover the bags and steep for 10 minutes. Take out the spent bags and fill with cold water to the batch size.
