# Fresh-Pressed WA Apple Cider with Ginger

## Yield
1 gallon

## Ingredients
- 1 gallon apple cider (ask culinary where it is located)
- 3/4 cup grated fresh ginger (fresh ginger is in the produce walk-in)

## Method
1. For each gallon of apple cider, add 3/4 cup grated fresh ginger.
2. Let the ginger macerate in the cider for at least 30 minutes.
3. Strain the mixture through a cone sieve, pressing on the ginger solids.

## Notes
- Scale linearly: 3/4 cup grated fresh ginger per gallon of apple cider.
