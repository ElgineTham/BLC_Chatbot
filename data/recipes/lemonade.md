# Lemonade

## Yield
1 gallon

## Ingredients
- 7 oz lemon concentrate
- Water to fill to 1 gallon

## Method
1. Pour 7 oz of lemon concentrate into a clean 1-gallon container.
2. Add water until the total volume reaches 1 gallon.
3. Stir well until fully mixed.
4. Taste and adjust if needed before service.

## Notes
- Ratio: 7 oz lemon concentrate per 1 gallon finished lemonade.
- Scale linearly for larger batches (e.g. 2 gallons → 14 oz concentrate).
