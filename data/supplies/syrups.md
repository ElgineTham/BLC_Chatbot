# Syrups

## Primary location
Above where the lemons are, inside the beverage cooler, near the sink by the coffee machine.

## Backup location
If there are none there, check inside the beverage cooler with the concentrates — on the blue racks.

## How to pull stock
1. Use opened ones first.
2. If none are open, take new ones from the boxes on the rack.

## Flavors
- Mango
- Strawberry
- Peach
