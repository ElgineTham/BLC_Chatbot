# Concentrates

## Location
Beverage cooler, on the blue racks.

## How to pull stock
1. Use opened ones first.
2. If none are open, take new ones from the boxes on the same racks.

## Flavors
- Pomegranate
- Lemonade
- Orange

## Notes
- Concentrates live on the blue racks inside the beverage cooler.
- Prefer open containers before opening a new box.
